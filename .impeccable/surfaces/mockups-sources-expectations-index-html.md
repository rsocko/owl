## Scope and mode

Operate-mode prototype for Paperless-anchored document-source review, separate external
signal reconciliation, durable expectation policy, and generalized document coverage.

## Audience and job

A single household operator must understand why OWL grouped documents, decide whether
future documents should be expected, define when absence matters, and monitor coverage
without learning internal persistence terminology.

## Task and proof

The primary task is to resolve one source and advance. Representative documents,
series evidence, plain-language monitoring consequences, and a durable policy summary
must remain visible enough to support the decision.

## Direction

Use OWL's established restrained blue-gray operating world. Organize the work into a
prioritized needs-review rail, a separate Tyrion signal mode, an all-correspondents directory,
a central evidence and series lane, and one focused policy panel. Use an explicit filter
popover for review reason, source disposition, evidence, and system relationships. Source
dispositions include expectations configured, no series expected, waiting for history, and
ignored or retired; event-driven evidence changes reopen review. Move generalized operational
monitoring to Document Coverage, with Statements retained as a category filter. Preserve
Statement Series Detail as the membership editor.

## Memorable moment

The recommended expectation is expressed as one readable sentence before any advanced
policy fields appear, and the setup flow continuously translates choices into the durable
policy OWL will store.

## Constraints and unresolved decisions

Paperless remains authoritative for documents and native metadata. Tyrion accounts and
obligations remain advisory relationships and never become document sources by themselves.
The prototype does not decide final production routing or data contracts.
